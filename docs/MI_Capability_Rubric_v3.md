## Cross-discipline rules
1. **Equivalency clause:** demonstrated capability can substitute for a credential at Scott's discretion (documented in the person's record) — credentials anchor, they don't gate absolutely. MI's own belt/Academy completions count as qualifying education where bodies allow (e.g., PD hours).
2. **Level is per discipline.** A PMP Senior may be a BA Associate. Pay rate for an assignment follows the level in the discipline being delivered.
3. **Currency:** external certs must be active where the body requires renewal (PMP 60 PDU/3 yrs; CBAP 60 CDU/3 yrs; CCMP 60 PDU/3 yrs).
4. **MI-belt bridge (LSS):** MI Yellow→Associate, Green→Practitioner, Black→Senior; Lead is earned by portfolio, not exam.
